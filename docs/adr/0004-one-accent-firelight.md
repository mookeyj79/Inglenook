# One accent: firelight

Inglenook has one accent, firelight amber, and it means "you are here": focus, cursor, selection, links, and primary buttons. It is not used for success, branding, or decoration.

One fixed accent keeps every app recognizably the same theme, and a warm amber suits a room lit by the fire. Keeping it to one meaning means a user's eye learns that amber is where they are.

## Consequences

- No accent picker and no per-port accent.
- `on_accent` is checked at 4.5:1 on firelight in both variants.
- Selections use a firelight wash, so text stays readable on them.
